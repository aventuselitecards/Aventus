The shop

About

Aventus is a collector-run shop. We buy cards we would hold ourselves: honest centering, clean surfaces, and prices that make sense on both sides of the deal. What you see on this page is live inventory, not a stock-photo catalog.

Graded and raw

PSA, BGS, and CGC slabs alongside carefully described raw singles. Authentication stays with the grader; condition notes stay with us.

Packed to arrive

Toploaders, team bags, and rigid mailers. Insurance is available on request. We ship the way we would want a package to show up.

Direct

Questions go to the person who listed the card. If a player is not up, ask — we source lots and singles.
